MWOS Android Access Guide

Use the web portal
1. Open https://tmc-copino-mwos.vercel.app/portal in your browser.
2. Sign in with your clinic account or create a patient account.
3. No installation is needed for the web portal.

Native app availability
A reviewed Android installer is not included in this download center.
This guide is not an app installer. Native releases require a platform build and testing; Apple platforms also require Apple signing for distribution.
