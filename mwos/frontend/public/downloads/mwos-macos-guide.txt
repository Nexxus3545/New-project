MWOS macOS Web App

Setup: https://tmc-copino-mwos.vercel.app/download#macos
Open portal: https://tmc-copino-mwos.vercel.app/portal

1. Open the setup page in Safari on macOS Sonoma (14) or later, or Chrome.
2. In Safari choose File > Add to Dock > Add. In Chrome choose Install MWOS when available or use the browser install menu.
3. Open MWOS from your Dock or Applications and sign in. On older Safari versions, open the portal directly or use Chrome.

Use your existing patient or clinic staff account. Internet is required for appointments, records, messages, and telehealth.
This installs the MWOS web app. Native APK, EXE, and App Store releases are not published here. If installation is not offered, open the portal directly in your browser.
