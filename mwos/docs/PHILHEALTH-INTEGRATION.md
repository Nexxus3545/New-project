# PhilHealth payment status in MWOS

Administrators and doctors open **Billing & Payments**, select a bill, and use **Update PhilHealth status**. Staff verify their access using a passkey before saving a protected change.

| Status | What it tells the patient |
| --- | --- |
| Not recorded | The clinic has yet to record a PhilHealth payment status for this bill. |
| In process | The clinic is waiting for the payment to be completed. |
| Paid to clinic | The clinic recorded that it received the PhilHealth payment. |

The most recent status change appears on the bill. Patients see their own bills and balances under **My Billing** on the web and on Android under **Profile**. Updating a status records the clinic's confirmation and adds an audit entry. Staff record money actually received through the existing payment workflow so the balance reflects the ledger.

Claims preparation was retired. Historical billing data is retained for existing records.
